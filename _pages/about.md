I plan to use this website in at least three ways: firstly, I'd like to have a place to collect the research I've done so far during my PhD. Secondly, I would like to have a place to post math-related projects that might not be suitable for a paper. Finally, I kind of want to write short explainers of concepts that I found confusing at some point in order to record how my understanding of it has changed.

To elaborate on the second point: there are some project ideas that I wanted to pursue at some point but could not due to time constraints. However, AI has significantly decreased the speed of executing one's ideas, and I hope I can take advantage of this advancement in some small way here. Moreover, some of those ideas might not be suitable for publication but are still interesting enough (to me) to spend a few days on; it would be nice to collect them here in an easy-to-access format.

The reason I chose the URL <twelve-month-winter.github.io> is that I wish the temperature were forever hovering around freezing with snowstorms every few weeks, with light snowfall every day. In "summer", sun should be replaced by rain and thunderstorms, but hypothetically sunshine is allowed provided that the UV index is below 2.

This website is very much under construction (published 11/17/2026).
